# Emberwild: Age of Wings — Player guide

## The short version

1. Press **Play Dragon Rush** on Home. Steer with the mouse, click to dash through glowing chests and pods, press **E** when your ability meter is full.
2. After the flight, feed your dragon Emberfruit in **Dragons** to level it up.
3. Every few minutes of play earns an **Aether Key**. Open eggs in the **Hatchery** to find new dragons.
4. Try **Sky Trials** for medals and **Dragon Arena** for a fight, both from **Play**.
5. The **Next goal** bar at the bottom of every screen always tells you something useful to do.

## Reading the world

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Glowing cracks (chests, pods, frozen rocks) | Breakable: dash through |
| Solid mossy stone | Fly around it (hitting it slows you briefly; you never lose resources in Rush) |
| Luminous rings | Good: fly through them |
| Pulsing amber column / red ground ring or lane | Danger is coming: move to the clear gap or out of the marked area |

## Dragon Rush

60-second flights (120 s **Expeditions** unlock after 10 full flights). The course scrolls by itself; you control height and dashes.

- Coins = gold, pink fruit = Emberfruit. Chests hold 8 gold, pods 6 Emberfruit.
- Every pickup charges **Dragon Rush**; when full you get 6 seconds of golden flight that pulls in everything nearby. Its own bonus trail can't recharge it.
- **Regions** unlock with full flights: Sunset Canyon (4) with wind rings that charge Rush, Prism Coast (8) with hanging pods, Frostglass Reach (12) with chest corridors, Stormspire (16) with telegraphed lightning.
- Ending a flight early from the pause menu banks what you collected. Reloading the page mid-flight loses that flight's haul.

## Sky Trials

12 designed courses in three groups (Sunleaf Isles; Canyon & Coast; Frost & Storm). Finishing a course unlocks the next.

- **Bronze**: reach the finish. **Silver** and **Gold**: collect enough rings with few or no collisions (each course lists its exact numbers).
- All dragons fly with the same level-1 stats here: levels and talents never change a medal. Your dragon's ability still works (its Trials effect is described in Dragons).
- First Bronze, Silver and Gold on each course give one-time rewards (40 gold, 25 Essence, 1 Aether Key). Repeats give a small amount of gold based on course length.
- **Practice** runs earn nothing and set no records. Leaving a course early or reloading ends the attempt without a medal.

## Dragon Arena

A top-down survival session of about four minutes: four waves, an elite in wave 4, and the Stormcrown Wyrm boss.

- Move with W A S D; attacks fire automatically at the nearest enemy; Space dashes (you are invulnerable during the dash); E fires your ability.
- Enemies: Bramble Grubs (slow), Dart Wisps (fast, fragile), Spore Turrets (their core glows before they fire), Plated Rams (flash red and show a lane before charging) and Treasure Imps (run away; carry lots of loot).
- After each wave the game pauses and you choose one of three upgrades for this session.
- You have health here. After a hit you are briefly invulnerable. If you fall, everything you collected and the XP you earned are still banked.
- Defeating the boss banks everything immediately (including a victory bonus). You can then continue into **endless** for a survival record; anything you collect there is banked when you stop or fall.

## Growing your dragons

- XP comes from active play (about 200 per minute in any mode) and from feeding **20 Emberfruit = 100 XP**.
- Levels go from 1 to 30. Dragons evolve at **5** (juvenile), **15** (adult) and **30** (elder). Level 5 strengthens the ability, level 15 makes it charge faster, level 30 grants the species title.
- **Talents** at levels 10 and 20: pick one of two small utility bonuses; you can change them freely between runs.
- Level bonuses are small and capped: up to +15% banked gold and fruit in Rush and Arena, and a little Arena damage and health.
- **Mastery**: three challenges per species (use its ability, a role challenge, then a Trial medal or Arena elite). Rewards are cosmetic: an alternate ability color, Radiant markings and a title — plus 1 Aether Key for all three.
- In **Dragons → Style** you can rename a dragon, mark favorites, choose markings and titles.

## Getting new dragons

- **Guaranteed routes**: Tide, Grove and Gale can be hatched with gold/Emberfruit (Grove and Gale need a lifetime achievement first). Bramble, Zephyr, Rime, Snowcap and Spark are guaranteed milestone rewards from contracts, wind rings, Sky Trials, levels and the Arena.
- **Essence catalog**: unlock any dragon directly with Essence (Common 40, Rare 100, Epic 240, Legendary 600).
- **Aether Hatchery**: each egg awards one dragon.

### The Aether Hatchery

- Keys come only from play: about **one per 3 minutes** of active play in any mode (the meter carries over between runs; paused time and menus don't count), plus one-time rewards from milestones, first medals and full mastery. You also start with one welcome key.
- **Odds**: Common 55%, Rare 28%, Epic 14%, Legendary 3%, then an equal chance for every species in that tier. The Hatchery shows the exact odds for your next egg and every species' chance.
- **Bad-luck protection**: the 10th egg in a row without an Epic or better is guaranteed Epic or Legendary (14 : 3); the 40th egg in a row without a Legendary is guaranteed Legendary.
- A dragon you already own becomes Essence: Common 10, Rare 25, Epic 60, Legendary 150.
- The result is decided and saved before the reel starts; the animation only shows it. **Skip** reveals it at once; if the page closes mid-reel, the Hatchery shows the same result next time without charging again.

## Contracts and the Journal

Three contracts are active at a time (from any mode). Each pays once; one free replacement recharges whenever you complete a contract. The Journal also lists milestones, your records, cosmetics (trails, perches, banners) and a short guide.

## Accessibility

Settings: separate volumes, mute, **reduced motion** (no screen shake, calmer menus, short fade instead of the egg reel), **low effects** (fewer particles), **auto-activate ability**, and collision outlines. All menus work with the keyboard. Rarity is always shown with a symbol and a word, not color alone.
